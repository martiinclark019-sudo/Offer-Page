Two methods, two questions

Purity tells you how clean. Identity tells you what.

High-performance liquid chromatography

The sample is pushed through a column that separates each component. A UV detector records them as peaks over time, and the target's peak area against the total gives the purity percentage.

  • Quantifies the target against everything else detected
  • Reveals impurities as separate, measurable peaks
